Word Hunt

How to Play

Unscramble the 9 letters to find as many words as you can. Words can be 3-9 letters long.

Scoring: You earn 1 point per letter in a word you find.

Controls

  • Type a word and click Guess or Enter on the keyboard to submit a guess.
  • To end the game and reveal the remain words click Give Up or Escape on the keyboard.
